Land Registration Search Telangana: The Complete Guide

Registration is the official recording of a transaction. In Telangana, checking registration details is crucial to ensure you are buying a property with a clean history and valid documents.

Since 2020, agricultural land registration is on Dharani, while non-agricultural properties (houses, plots) remain with IGRS.

1. The Land Registration Process (Step-by-Step)

Before you search for records, understand how a registration happens:

Step A: Pre-Registration Checks

  • Check ROR 1B (Ownership).
  • Check EC (Encumbrances).
  • Check Prohibited List (Sec 22A).

Step B: Slot Booking

  • Book a slot online on Dharani (Agri) or IGRS (Non-Agri).
  • Pay Stamp Duty (approx 5.5% to 7.5% depending on area/gender) and Registration Fees online.
  • Upload documents.

Step C: Physical Registration

  • Visit the SRO/Tahsildar office on the scheduled date.
  • Biometric verification (Thumb/Iris) of Buyer, Seller, and Witnesses.
  • Photo capture.
  • Instant generation of Document Number.

2. How to Search for Registered Documents?

A. Search by Document Number

If you have the document number (e.g., 2045/2022):

  1. Go to IGRS Website > Certified Copy.
  2. Enter District, SRO Name, Document No, and Year.
  3. You will see the details of the parties involved.

B. Search by Names (Not Publicly Available)

For privacy, you generally cannot search all properties owned by a specific person name (e.g., "Show me all lands of Ravi Kumar"). You must have property details.

C. Card Search (Agricultural)

On Dharani, you can search "Registered Document Details" by entering the District, Mandal, and Document Year/Number to verify if a specific deed is genuine.

3. Documents Required for Registration

Ensure you have these ready before booking a slot:

Document Purpose
Sale Deed Draft The legal agreement outlining terms of sale.
EC (Encumbrance Certificate) Proof of clear title history.
Property Tax Receipt Proof that taxes are paid up to date (for houses).
Pattadar Passbook Mandatory for agricultural land sellers.
Identity Proofs Aadhar & PAN Card of Buyer, Seller, and 2 Witnesses.
Section 22A Clearance Only if the land was previously in a disputed category.

రిజిస్ట్రేషన్ కి వెళ్ళే ముందు జాగ్రత్తలు:

  • లింక్ డాక్యుమెంట్స్: అమ్మే వ్యక్తికి ఆ ఆస్తి ఎలా వచ్చిందో తెలిపే పాత డాక్యుమెంట్స్ (Link Docs) అడిగి తీసుకోండి.
  • మార్కెట్ విలువ (Market Value): ప్రభుత్వం నిర్ణయించిన రేటు ప్రకారమే స్టాంప్ డ్యూటీ కట్టాలి. తక్కువ కడితే భవిష్యత్తులో "అండర్ వాల్యుయేషన్" (Under Valuation) నోటీసులు వస్తాయి.
  • స్లాట్ బుకింగ్: స్లాట్ బుక్ చేసుకోకుండా నేరుగా రిజిస్ట్రార్ ఆఫీసుకి వెళ్ళకండి. పని జరగదు.

ఫేక్ డాక్యుమెంట్లను గుర్తించడం ఎలా?

IGRS లేదా ధరణి వెబ్‌సైట్ లో "Document Search" లోకి వెళ్లి, మీ దగ్గర ఉన్న డాక్యుమెంట్ నెంబర్ ఎంటర్ చేయండి. అక్కడ వచ్చే వివరాలు (పేర్లు, విస్తీర్ణం) మీ పేపర్ లో ఉన్న వాటితో సరిపోలాలి.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I do registration anywhere in Telangana?

For agricultural lands under Dharani, you can register at the Tahsildar office of that specific mandal OR usually at adjoining mandals (subject to current rules). For non-agri, you generally go to the jurisdictional SRO.

2. What happens if I lose my original Sale Deed?

Submit an FIR to the police immediately. Then, apply for a "Certified Copy (CC)" of the deed from the registration office. The CC has the same legal value as the original for most purposes.

3. Is PAN Card mandatory for buying land?

Yes, if the property value exceeds ₹10 Lakhs (the limit varies but strictly enforced for high value), quoting PAN is mandatory for tax purposes.

4. How much is the Stamp Duty?

It varies, but typically it is around 7.5% of the market value (Registration fee + Stamp Duty + Transfer Duty). Always verify the latest rates on the IGRS portal calculator.
