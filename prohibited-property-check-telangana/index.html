What is Prohibited Property in Telangana?

Prohibited property refers to land parcels that cannot be sold or registered due to legal, government, or court restrictions.

Why is land listed as prohibited?

  • Government lands
  • Court dispute lands
  • Forest lands
  • Endowment lands
  • Assigned lands

How to Check Prohibited Property Online in Telangana?

  1. Visit the official Bhu Bharati Telangana portal
  2. Login using OTP
  3. Select "Prohibited Property Search"
  4. Enter District, Mandal, Village, Survey Number
  5. Submit to view status
