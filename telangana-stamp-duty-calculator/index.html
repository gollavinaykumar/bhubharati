Stamp Duty & Registration Charges Calculator: 2026 Guide

Registering a property involves paying three components: Stamp Duty, Registration Fee, and Transfer Duty. These charges vary based on the type of transaction (Sale, Gift, Partition) and relationship between parties.

This guide provides a comprehensive breakdown of the current rates in Telangana.

1. Current Registration Charges Table (2026)

(Note: Rates are subject to change by Government Orders. Please verify on official IGRS portal.)

Document Type Stamp Duty Transfer Duty Registration Fee Total
Sale Deed 5.5% 1.5% 0.5% 7.5%
Gift Deed (Family) 1% - 3% (Varies) 1.5% 0.5% Lower than Sale
Partition / Settlement 1% (Capped) - ₹1000 Nominal
Release Deed 3% - 0.5% 3.5%
Mortgage (Deposit of Title) 0.5% (Capped at ₹50,000) 0.1% 0.1% ~0.7%

2. Simple Calculator Logic

To estimate your cost, follow this formula:

Total Cost = (Market Value OR Sale Price) × 7.5% + User Charges + Scanning Charges

Example Calculation:

  • Market Value: ₹20,00,000
  • Stamp Duty (5.5%): ₹1,10,000
  • Transfer Duty (1.5%): ₹30,000
  • Registration Fee (0.5%): ₹10,000
  • Total Payable: ₹1,50,000 (Plus approx ₹500 scan charges)

3. How to Pay the Fees?

Gone are the days of buying physical stamp papers from vendors for high values. Now, payments are digital.

  1. Generate Challan: Log in to IGRS/Dharani and generate a challan for the calculated amount.
  2. Online Payment: Pay via Net Banking, Credit/Debit Card, or UPI.
  3. T-Wallet / MeeSeva: You can also pay at MeeSeva centers if you cannot do it online.
  4. Receipt: Download the challan receipt. This is mandatory to show at the SRO on registration day.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I get a refund if I cancel the slot?

Yes. If you cancel the registration slot or the transaction doesn't happen, you can apply for a refund of Stamp Duty (within 6 months), subject to a deduction (usually 10%). Registration fees might not be fully refundable.

2. Is it better to buy in a specific month?

Technically no, but avoid registering during "Ashada Masam" if you believe in auspicious dates, as SROs are less crowded. However, rates are strictly government-fixed and don't change seasonally.

3. What is franking?

Franking is a method of paying stamp duty for loan documents or agreements where a machine stamps the document indicating duty paid. For property registration, the e-Challan / e-Stamp system is now preferred over franking.

4. What is the penalty for "Under Valuation"?

If you register a property below the government guideline value to save tax (Section 47A), the registrar can impound the document. You will have to pay the deficit duty plus a heavy penalty to release the document.
