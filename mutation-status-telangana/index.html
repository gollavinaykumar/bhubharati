Mutation అంటే ఏమిటి?

What is Land Mutation?

Land Mutation is the legal process of updating ownership details in government land records after a sale, inheritance, or transfer.

Why is Mutation Important?

  • Updates the new owner name in official records
  • Provides legal ownership proof
  • Avoids future property disputes
  • Required for bank loans and transactions

How to Check Mutation Status Online in Telangana?

  1. Visit the official Bhu Bharati Telangana website
  2. Login using your registered mobile number
  3. Select “Application Status”
  4. Enter your mutation application number
  5. View your approval or pending status

How Long Does Mutation Take?

Mutation approval usually takes between 15–30 days depending on verification process.

Mutation పూర్తయ్యాక ROR అప్డేట్ అవుతుందా?

అవును, Mutation పూర్తైన తర్వాత ROR రికార్డులో కొత్త యజమాని పేరు కనిపిస్తుంది.

What if mutation application is rejected?

You must contact the local Tahsildar office and resubmit required documents.
