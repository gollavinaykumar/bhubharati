Rythu Bandhu Scheme Telangana: Complete Guide (2026)

Last Updated: February 2026 | Editorial Policy

Rythu Bandhu (రైతు బంధు) is the Telangana government's flagship farmer welfare scheme that provides direct investment support to all Pattadar farmers for each agricultural season. It is one of the most ambitious farmer support programs in India.

Understanding how this scheme connects to your land records, Pattadar Passbook, and Dharani Portal is essential to ensure you receive your benefits without issues.

1. రైతు బంధు పథకం అంటే ఏమిటి? (What is Rythu Bandhu?)

రైతు బంధు అనేది తెలంగాణ రాష్ట్ర ప్రభుత్వం ప్రతి పట్టాదారు రైతుకు (భూమి యజమానికి) ఇచ్చే పెట్టుబడి సహాయం. ఈ డబ్బు విత్తనాలు, ఎరువులు, పురుగు మందులు కొనుగోలు చేయడానికి ఉద్దేశించబడింది.

Key Details at a Glance

Feature Details
Amount ₹5,000 per acre per season (₹10,000 per acre per year)
Seasons Vanakalam (Kharif - June) and Yasangi (Rabi - December)
Eligibility All Pattadar farmers owning agricultural land
Payment Mode Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) linked to Aadhar
Minimum Land No minimum — even owners of less than 1 acre receive proportionate benefits

2. Eligibility Criteria (అర్హత నిబంధనలు)

To receive Rythu Bandhu, you must satisfy ALL of the following:

  • Pattadar Status: You must be a registered Pattadar in the ROR 1B records. Tenants and cultivators without title DO NOT qualify.
  • Agricultural Land: Your land must be classified as "Agricultural" (Wet or Dry) in the ROR. NALA-converted land is NOT eligible.
  • Updated Passbook: Your Green Pattadar Passbook must be updated with correct details (name, extent, survey number).
  • Aadhar-Bank Linking: Your Aadhar number must be seeded (linked) with your bank account for DBT.
  • Mutation Complete: If you recently bought the land, mutation must be completed before you can receive benefits.

ఎవరికి అర్హత లేదు?

  • ప్రభుత్వ ఉద్యోగులు (Central or State Government employees)
  • ఇన్‌కమ్ ట్యాక్స్ కట్టే వ్యక్తులు (Income tax payees — subject to income threshold)
  • అసైన్డ్ భూమి ఉన్న వ్యక్తులు (in some cases, subject to verification)
  • NALA కన్వర్షన్ అయిన భూమి యజమానులు

3. How to Check Rythu Bandhu Status Online?

  1. Visit the official Rythu Bandhu Portal or the Dharani Portal
  2. Navigate to "Rythu Bandhu" or "Farmer Benefits" section
  3. Enter your Aadhar Number or Pattadar Passbook Number
  4. The system will show:
    • Your Pattadar Name and Extent of land
    • Eligible Amount for the current season
    • Payment Status (Credited / Pending / Rejected)
    • Bank Account details (last 4 digits)

4. రైతు బంధు రాకపోతే ఏం చేయాలి? (What If Payment is Not Received?)

రైతు బంధు డబ్బులు రాకపోవడానికి చాలా కారణాలు ఉంటాయి:

సమస్య పరిష్కారం
ఆధార్ బ్యాంకు అకౌంట్ లింక్ కాలేదు బ్యాంకుకి వెళ్లి Aadhar Seeding చేయించుకోండి
మ్యూటేషన్ (Mutation) పూర్తి కాలేదు Dharani లో మ్యూటేషన్ స్టేటస్ చెక్ చేయండి
పాస్‌బుక్ లో పేరు తప్పుగా ఉంది Dharani "Data Correction" దరఖాస్తు పెట్టండి
భూమి నిషేధిత (22A) జాబితాలో ఉంది 22A నుండి తొలగింపు కోసం Grievance పెట్టండి

5. Connection to Land Records

Rythu Bandhu is deeply connected to your land records on the Dharani Portal. Here's why keeping your records updated is essential:

  • ROR 1B is the source of truth: The Rythu Bandhu system pulls your Pattadar name and land extent directly from the ROR 1B records. If your name is missing or wrong there, you won't receive the benefit.
  • Mutation triggers eligibility: After buying land, you become eligible for Rythu Bandhu only AFTER mutation is completed and your name appears in the ROR.
  • EC validates ownership: In disputed cases, the system may check Encumbrance Certificates to validate ownership claims.
  • Survey Number accuracy: Benefits are calculated per acre. If the extent in the survey number records is incorrect, your payment amount will be wrong.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do both husband and wife get Rythu Bandhu if land is jointly owned?

No. Rythu Bandhu is paid per acre, not per person. If 2 acres is jointly owned, the total benefit is for 2 acres distributed among the joint holders as per their share in the records.

2. I sold my land last month. Will I still get Rythu Bandhu?

No. Once mutation transfers ownership to the buyer, benefits shift to the new Pattadar from the next season.

3. Is Rythu Bandhu taxable?

Rythu Bandhu income is generally treated as agricultural income and is exempt from income tax for individual farmers. However, consult a CA for specific situations.

4. Can tenants (Kauludarlu) get Rythu Bandhu?

No. Only Pattadar (title-holding) landowners are eligible. Tenants must approach the landowner for support.

5. Why Rythu Bandhu is linked to land records?

Rythu Bandhu uses ROR 1B and Pattadar Passbook data from Dharani. Your name and extent must be correct there. Mutation after purchase must be done; otherwise the previous owner may still get the benefit. రైతు బంధు ధరణి ROR 1B మరియు పాస్ బుక్ డేటా ఉపయోగిస్తుంది; మ్యూటేషన్ పూర్తి అయ్యేవరకు మునుపటి యజమానికి బెనిఫిట్ వెళ్లవచ్చు.

6. What if my Aadhar is not linked to passbook?

Payment is through DBT (Aadhar-linked bank account). If Aadhar is not linked or bank details are wrong, payment will fail. Update linkage at MeeSeva or through Dharani grievance. ఆధార్ లింక్ లేకుంటే డిబిటి రాదు; మీసేవ/ధరణి ద్వారా నవీకరించండి.

7. Is there a maximum land ceiling for Rythu Bandhu?

No ceiling. All Pattadar farmers receive ₹5,000 per acre per season regardless of total extent. Large landowners also get the same rate per acre. గరిష్ట పరిమితి లేదు; ఎకరాకు ₹5,000 ప్రతి సీజన్.

8. What are Vanakalam and Yasangi?

Vanakalam (Kharif) is the monsoon crop season — payment usually in June. Yasangi (Rabi) is the winter crop season — payment usually in December. వనకాలం — ఖరీఫ్ (జూన్); యాసంగి — రబీ (డిసెంబర్).

9. Can I get Rythu Bandhu for converted (NALA) land?

No. Once land is converted to non-agricultural (NALA), it is no longer eligible for Rythu Bandhu. NALA మార్పిడి అయిన భూమికి రైతు బంధు అర్హత లేదు.

10. Where to complain if payment is not received?

Use the official Rythu Bandhu portal status check first. If the land is eligible but payment failed, contact the designated helpline or approach the MRO with passbook and Aadhar. రైతు బంధు పోర్టల్ స్టేటస్ చెక్ చేయండి; ఆ తర్వాత హెల్ప్‌లైన్ లేదా MRO సంప్రదించండి.

Troubleshooting (పరిష్కారాలు)

English: If you are not receiving Rythu Bandhu: (1) Verify your name and extent on Dharani ROR 1B and passbook. (2) Ensure mutation is complete if you bought recently. (3) Check Aadhar–bank account linkage. (4) Confirm land is not in prohibited list or NALA-converted. (5) Check status on the official Rythu Bandhu portal with Aadhar or passbook number. తెలుగు: రైతు బంధు రాకపోతే ధరణిలో పేరు మరియు విస్తీర్ణం, మ్యూటేషన్, ఆధార్-బ్యాంక్ లింక్, నిషేధిత జాబితా చెక్ చేయండి.
