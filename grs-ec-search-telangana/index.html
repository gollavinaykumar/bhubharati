IGRS Telangana EC Search: The Complete Guide (Non-Agri)

While Dharani handles agricultural lands, the Registration & Stamps Department (IGRS) is responsible for all other properties in Telangana—such as independent houses, apartments, and open plots.

If you are buying a plot in a layout or a flat in a city, checking the IGRS EC is mandatory to ensure there are no legal dues or double registrations.

1. How to Check EC on IGRS Portal?

Follow these steps to search for Encumbrance Certificates for non-agricultural properties:

Step 1: Go to Official Portal

  • Visit IGRS Telangana.
  • Click on "Encumbrance Search (EC)" in the right sidebar.

Step 2: Login / Citizen Registration

  • Unlike the public search earlier, you may now need to log in as a citizen.
  • Register with your mobile number if required.

Step 3: Search Criteria

You can search using:

  • Document Number: The easiest way. Enter the Registration Document No (e.g., 2540/2019) and SRO Name.
  • Property Details: If you don't have the doc number, enter House No, Ward, Block, and Locality.
Note: Searching by name (e.g., "Ravi Kumar") is not officially supported to protect privacy. You must have property details.

2. Understanding IGRS vs Dharani

It is crucial to know which portal to use:

Feature IGRS Portal Dharani Portal
Property Type Plots, Houses, Flats, Commercial Buildings Agricultural Land only
Identifier House No, Plot No, Survey No (Urban) Survey No, Passbook No
Department Registration & Stamps Revenue Department

IGRS EC సెర్చ్ (ఇళ్లు మరియు ప్లాట్ల కోసం)

వ్యవసాయ భూములకు ధరణి ఎలాగో, ఇళ్లు మరియు ఖాళీ ప్లాట్లకు IGRS (రిజిస్ట్రేషన్ శాఖ) అలా పనిచేస్తుంది.

మీ ప్లాట్ సేఫ్ గా ఉందని తెలుసుకోవడం ఎలా?

IGRS వెబ్‌సైట్ లో "Encumbrance Search" ద్వారా మీ ప్లాట్ పైన గత 30 ఏళ్లలో జరిగిన లావాదేవీలు (Link Documents) అన్నీ చూడవచ్చు. ఒకవేళ ఆ ప్లాట్ వేరే వారి పేరున తనఖా (Mortgage) పెట్టి ఉంటే, అది కూడా EC లో కనిపిస్తుంది.

IGRS లో ఏమి దొరుకుతాయి?

  • ఓపెన్ ప్లాట్స్ (Open Plots): లేఅవుట్ లోని ప్లాట్ వివరాలు.
  • అపార్ట్మెంట్ ఫ్లాట్స్ (Apartments): ఫ్లాట్ నెంబర్ మరియు అన్-డివైడెడ్ షేర్ (UDS) వివరాలు.
  • ఇళ్లు (Independent Houses): ఇంటి నెంబర్ ఆధారంగా రిజిస్ట్రేషన్ హిస్టరీ.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What does "Statement on Encumbrance" mean?

This is the formal name for an EC. It traces the history of ownership transfers and liabilities (mortgages) on a property over a specified period.

2. Can I get IGRS EC for agricultural land?

Generally, NO. Since 2020, agricultural records moved to Dharani. However, for old transactions (pre-Dharani) on agri lands, you might still find historical data in IGRS archives.

3. Why is the EC showing "Nil"?

A "Nil EC" means there were no transactions found in the searching period. However, be careful—it could also mean you entered the wrong details. Always double-check SRO and Survey Numbers.

4. How to find the SRO for my area?

Use the "Know Your SRO" option on the IGRS website. Entering your Mandal and Village will show which Sub-Registrar Office has jurisdiction over your property.
