Document Search (Certified Copy) Telangana: Ultimate Guide

Losing original property documents can be a nightmare. However, the Registration Department maintains detailed records of every registered transaction. The Certified Copy (CC) is your safety net.

Whether you need to verify a seller claims or replace a lost deed, downloading a CC is the solution. This guide covers how to access these documents online.

1. Why Do You Need a Certified Copy (CC)?

A CC is required in several critical scenarios:

  • Lost Original Deed: If you misplace your sale deed, a CC acts as the legal substitute.
  • Link Verification: Before buying, you must read the *previous* sale deeds (Link Documents) to verify how the current seller acquired the property.
  • Bank Loans: Banks mandate the submission of CCs of link documents for the last 13-30 years to approve mortgage loans.
  • Court Disputes: CCs are admissible evidence in court cases regarding property disputes.

2. How to Download Certified Copy Online?

Method A: IGRS Portal (For Open Plots, Houses, Flats)

  1. Visit IGRS Telangana.
  2. Go to the "Certified Copy" section.
  3. You will be asked to verify your identity (Mobile OTP).
  4. Enter the required details:
    • District & SRO.
    • Document No (e.g., 540).
    • Year (e.g., 2018).
  5. Pay the required fee online (User charges).
  6. Download the digitally signed PDF.

Method B: Dharani Portal (For Agricultural Land)

  1. Login to Dharani.
  2. Select "Agriculture".
  3. Navigate to "Search Registered Document".
  4. Enter details and pay the fee to view or download the deed.
Tip: If you don't know the document number, first perform an EC Search (Encumbrance Certificate) using the Survey Number. The EC will list all Document Numbers associated with that land.

అమ్మే వ్యక్తి "ఇది నా భూమి" అని చెప్పొచ్చు. కానీ ఆ భూమి అతనికి ఎలా వచ్చింది? అతను ఎవరి దగ్గర కొన్నాడు? ఆ డాక్యుమెంట్లో ఏవైనా షరతులు ఉన్నాయా? ఇవన్నీ తెలియాలంటే మీరు పాత డాక్యుమెంట్లు (Link Documents) డౌన్లోడ్ చేసి చదవాలి.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I get a CC of a Will Deed?

No. Will Deeds and other testamentary documents are confidential during the lifetime of the testator. They are not open to the public for search unless processed after death.

2. How much does a CC cost?

The online download fee is nominal (often around ₹200-₹500 depending on pages and service charges). Getting a physical copy from SRO might involve per-page stamping charges.

3. What if the CC is blurred or unreadable?

Sometimes, older scanned documents are blurry. In such cases, you must visit the respective SRO involved and request a fresh scan or a manual copy from the bound volumes.

4. Does CC include the map/plan?

Yes, a complete Certified Copy includes the main deed content as well as the attached schedule plans (maps) and photographs of the parties.
