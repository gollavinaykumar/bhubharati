Telanganaలో డాక్యుమెంట్ సెర్చ్ ఎలా చేయాలి?

  1. 👉 IGRS Telangana Portal ఓపెన్ చేయండి

  3. District & SRO Office ఎంపిక చేయండి

What is Land Registration Document Search?

This service allows users to check registered property documents such as sale deeds, gift deeds, and mortgage deeds online.

You can view document number, registration details, property owner name, and transaction information.
In Content Ad Space

Steps to Search Telangana Registered Documents Online

  1. Visit the official IGRS Telangana website
  2. Choose “Document Details Search”
  3. Select District and Sub-Registrar Office (SRO)
  4. Enter Document Number and Year
  5. View registered sale deed or property document details
